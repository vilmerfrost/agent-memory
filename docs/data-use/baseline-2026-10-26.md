Sequence findings:

- **S1. Skill scores are a day old at night.** rsi-nightly (03:30) refreshes `skill_scores` after amem-rsi-night (01:30)
  has picked its candidates. Newest `skill_scores` row: 2026-10-26 02:30 UTC (03:30 CET). The night of 2026-10-26
  therefore selects skill candidates from scores computed about 22 hours earlier.
- **S2. No sync ran on 2026-10-26.** amem-daily moved from 09:20 to 00:40, but its new plist was installed at
  01:37 CET on 2026-10-26, after that day's 00:40 slot. The old 09:20 slot no longer exists. Its first run on the new
  schedule is 2026-10-27 00:40. `amem sync status`: ClickHouse, Langfuse and past.dev all show
  `last_ok=2026-10-25T08:26Z`. None of the 2026-10-25 night's experiments are in any external sink.
- **S3. From 2026-10-27 the order works.** A night writes its experiments at about 01:30-01:55. The next
  amem-daily at 00:40 syncs them to the external sinks before the following night starts at 01:30. So night
  *N+1* can read night *N*'s traces and decisions. S2 was a one-time gap caused by the schedule move, not a
  standing ordering problem. A night can never read its own experiments from the sinks; it reads them from
  Supabase.
